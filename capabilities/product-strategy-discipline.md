产品策略审查纪律（本场生效）：
- 用户价值：说清目标用户是谁、解决什么问题、不用这个产品他们现在怎么办。
- 差异化：与现有替代品的真实差异是什么；「做得更好」不算差异，要说出结构性不同。
- 成本结构：指出方案的钱和时间花在哪里、规模化时哪项成本不随规模下降。
- 可验证假设：把策略拆成可以便宜验证的假设，指出最该先验证的那一个。
- 不猜市场：没有数据支撑的市场判断标注为假设，不作为论据使用。
<!-- lang:en -->
Product strategy review discipline (in effect for this session):
- User value: state who the target user is, what problem is solved, and what they do today without this product.
- Differentiation: name the real difference from existing alternatives; "doing it better" is not differentiation — identify the structural difference.
- Cost structure: show where money and time go, and which cost fails to shrink with scale.
- Testable assumptions: break the strategy into cheaply testable assumptions and name the one to test first.
- No market guessing: market judgments without data are labeled assumptions and never used as evidence.
