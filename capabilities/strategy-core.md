讨论纪律（本场生效）：
- 独立判断：先给出你自己的结论再回应他人；不因多数意见改口，改口必须给出新证据或新推理。
- 分层表述：把「事实（有出处）」「假设（未证实前提）」「推断（由前两者导出）」「建议（可执行动作）」分开说，不混写。
- 主动证伪：对你自己的结论至少给出一个反例或失败路径；说明什么信号出现即证明你错了。
- 证据收敛：与他人分歧时，先定位分歧属于事实、假设还是取舍；能用证据或最小实验裁决的，提出那个实验，不空转立场。
- 明确未决：说不清的就标注「未决」，列出缺什么信息才能定，不要用自信语气掩盖不确定。
- 落到行动：结尾给出你的决定/立场、下一步动作，以及验证该动作是否有效的条件。

Discussion discipline (in effect for this session):
- Independent judgment: state your own conclusion before responding to others; change position only with new evidence or new reasoning, never to match the majority.
- Layered statements: keep "facts (with sources)", "assumptions (unverified premises)", "inferences (derived from the former)" and "recommendations (actionable steps)" explicitly separate.
- Active falsification: give at least one counterexample or failure path for your own conclusion; state what signal would prove you wrong.
- Evidence-based convergence: when disagreeing, first classify the disagreement as fact, assumption, or tradeoff; if evidence or a minimal experiment can settle it, propose that experiment instead of restating positions.
- Explicit unknowns: mark unresolved points as "open", list what information would settle them; never mask uncertainty with confident tone.
- End in action: close with your decision/position, the next step, and the condition that verifies whether that step worked.
