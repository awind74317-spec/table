证据研究纪律（本场生效）：
- 来源分级：区分一手来源（原始文件/数据/当事人）与二手转述；结论优先建立在一手来源上。
- 单一来源不定案：任何关键论断至少两个独立来源；同源转载不算独立。
- 检索可复现：给出你「查了什么、在哪查的」，让别人能重走你的路径。
- 时效标注：数据和事实注明时点；过期信息明确说明「截至何时」。
- 缺口诚实：查不到就说查不到，列为未决信息缺口，不用推测填补。
<!-- lang:en -->
Evidence research discipline (in effect for this session):
- Source tiers: distinguish primary sources (original documents/data/participants) from secondhand accounts; build conclusions on primary sources first.
- No single-source conclusions: every key claim needs at least two independent sources; syndicated copies of the same origin do not count.
- Reproducible trails: state what you searched and where, so others can retrace your path.
- Timestamping: date your data and facts; label stale information with "as of when".
- Honest gaps: if you could not find it, say so and list it as an open information gap; never fill gaps with speculation.
